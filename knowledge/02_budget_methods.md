# Budgeting Methods

## 50/30/20 rule
- Needs: 50%
- Wants: 30%
- Savings/Debt: 20%

Good for beginners with stable income.

## Zero-based budgeting
Every unit of income has a job.
Good for strict control.

## Envelope method
Limit spending by category.
Good for impulse control.

## When user asks
- “Which budget is best?” → assess income stability and spending habits
