# Expense Analysis Playbook

## Key insight
Most savings come from fixing the top 1–2 spending categories.

## Analysis steps
1) Rank expenses by total amount
2) Identify recurring vs one-time costs
3) Compare wants vs needs
4) Look for frequency traps (small daily spending)

## When user asks
- “Analyze my spending” → summarize + highlight biggest category
- “Where can I save?” → target the largest expense first
